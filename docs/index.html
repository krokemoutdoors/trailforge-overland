Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 28, 2026

TrailForge Overland keeps your vehicle build profile, gyroscope pitch/roll readings, and location history on your device. We do not track you across third-party apps, and we never sell location history to data brokers or advertising networks.

Anonymous regional requests go to public U.S. government endpoints (USGS Water Services, USDA SNOTEL, NIFC Wildfire Perimeters, and USFS roads). A trail scan sends the current map center or GPS coordinate to OpenStreetMap Overpass. Turn-by-turn routing sends the start and destination to Apple, and to the public OSRM driving service when Apple cannot return a road route. Spoken turn prompts stay on the phone.

If you publish a trail condition or hazard pin, TrailForge sends that coordinate, the status you picked, and your rig type to the shared community feed. The feed does not include your name. Reports stay queued on the device until a connection returns.
