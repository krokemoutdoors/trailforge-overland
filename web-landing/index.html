Privacy Policy (App Store Connect Verified)

Effective Date: October 25, 2026

TrailForge Overland ("the App") keeps your vehicle build profile, gyroscope pitch/roll readings, and location history on your iOS device. We do not track you across third-party apps, and we never sell location history to data brokers or advertising networks (NSPrivacyTracking = false).

Anonymous regional requests go to public U.S. government endpoints (USGS Water Services, USDA SNOTEL, NIFC Wildfire Perimeters, and USFS roads). When you tap a trail scan, the app also sends the current map center or GPS coordinate to the OpenStreetMap Overpass service to load public 4x4 tracks. Turn-by-turn routing sends that start and the chosen camp to Apple for driving directions, and to the public OSRM driving service when Apple cannot return a road route. Spoken turn prompts are generated on the phone and are not uploaded.

If you publish a trail condition or hazard pin, TrailForge sends that coordinate, the status you picked, and your rig type to the shared community feed so other drivers can see a closed gate or obstacle. The feed does not include your name. Reports stay queued on the device until a connection returns.
