## Full App Store Description
**Built for Overlanders, Not Bloated Social Feeds.**
Whether you drive a stock Subaru Outback Wilderness, a high-roof Sprinter 4x4 camper van, or a triple-locked Tacoma on 35s, **TrailForge Overland** tells you—before you leave pavement—whether your exact build will fit the trail and where to pitch a level camp before dark.

### 5 REASONS OVERLANDERS SWITCH TO TRAILFORGE:
1. **"Will My Exact Rig Fit?" Build-Matched Filtering**
Enter your tire size, lift, minimum differential clearance, roof/tent height, and lockers once. Every trail displays an instant **Fits Your Rig / Caution / No-Go** badge with low tree-canopy warnings, narrow shelf clearances, and max water-ford depths.
2. **Flat (<3° Slope) Dispersed Campsite & Starlink Finder**
Combine legal BLM & USFS dispersed camping boundaries with 1-meter LIDAR ground-slope shading. Spot level pull-outs (`<3°` slope—no leveling blocks required!) complete with Starlink clear-sky percentages, daily solar charging hours, and cell carrier bars.
3. **Live Gate, Snow & River-Crossing Telemetry**
Connected directly to live **USGS Streamflow gauges**, **USDA NRCS SNOTEL snow sensors**, **USFS MVUM seasonal gate dates**, and **NIFC active wildfire perimeters** so you never waste a 4-hour drive on a locked gate or flooded ford.
4. **Built-In Gyro Clinometer, Air-Down PSI Calculator & Offline Convoy Mesh**
Monitor real-time Pitch & Roll angles using your iPhone's gyroscope, compute exact off-road tire pressures for rock, sand, washboard, or snow, and share live peer-to-peer convoy locations with friends over Bluetooth/Wi-Fi with zero cell service.
5. **1-Tap Offline Topo Maps & GPX 1.1 Import/Export**
Download compact USGS topo + public land packs for dead-zone backcountry trips and import or export standard `.gpx` tracks and waypoints in one tap.
